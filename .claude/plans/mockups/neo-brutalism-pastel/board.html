Notisbook — proposta Neo-Brutalism pastel (cantos retos) — claro × escuro

Same layout, content and English labels as the current UI; only the visual language changes. Every item is shown twice: Claro (left) and Escuro (right). border-radius: 0 on everything, 2px/3px rules (black in light, soft off-white #CFC9BC in dark), solid zero-blur shadows by layer, a generous pastel palette; calm chrome: rail and side-panel headers on the app background, active tab = menubar surface, solid resource meter. Dark is soft everywhere: graphite bg #2B2A31 + 7% soft-white dots, surface #35343C, surface-2 #3E3D46, text #E8E4DA, muted #B3AEA3, shadows #1B1A20, softened pastels with #2A2930 text — no pure black or white; where the shadow disappears the cue is the #CFC9BC frame (dialogs, menus, buttons), a 3px soft-yellow outline (selected cell) or a #CFC9BC offset shadow (logos). Fonts: Archivo Black · Space Grotesk · Public Sans · JetBrains Mono. Icons: Tabler outline, square caps + miter joins.
